Fair Play

Tournament Rules

A legitimate stage needs real rules. This is the framework every BeyondGød tournament runs on — specifics per event are added once a format is locked.

1. Eligibility

Open to BGMI players [EDIT: confirm minimum age, e.g. 16+] with a valid, non-banned BGMI account in good standing. Players must compete under the same in-game name (IGN) submitted at registration — no IGN swapping mid-tournament without organiser approval.

2. Squad Composition

Squads consist of up to 5 players: 4 main roster + 1 substitute. A substitute may only play if declared before the match starts. Playing an undeclared or ineligible player results in that match's disqualification.

3. Fair Play & Anti-Cheat

  • Zero tolerance for hacking, scripting, macros, or any third-party software that alters gameplay.
  • Teaming with opposing squads, stream-sniping, and match-fixing are strictly banned.
  • Organisers may require screen recording or spectator access during matches. Refusal to comply may result in disqualification.
  • Any player caught cheating is banned from all future BeyondGød events, along with their squad's result for that match voided.

4. Code of Conduct

Harassment, hate speech, discrimination, or abusive behaviour toward other players, staff, or spectators — in-game, in Discord, or on stream — will not be tolerated and can result in immediate removal from the tournament.

6. Reporting & Disputes

Report rule violations to tournament staff via Discord with proof — screenshots or clips — within [EDIT: e.g. 30 minutes] of the match ending. Organiser decisions on disputes are final.

8. Amendments

BeyondGød reserves the right to amend these rules between events, and to disqualify any squad or player found violating the spirit of fair competition even where a specific rule isn't explicitly listed above.

This is a general rules framework, not a finalised tournament ruleset. Match-specific details (format, prize pool, timings) will be published separately for each event before registration opens for it.
