Who We Are

Players play. We build the stage.

BeyondGød was founded in 2024 by XYRØ with one goal: give BGMI players with real potential a fair shot, even when family support, guidance, or resources have stood in their way. We are not a competing roster — like Nodwin Gaming and similar organisers, we design, host, and run tournaments so that talent decides the outcome, not circumstance.

Mission

Find overlooked BGMI talent and give them a legitimate, well-run stage to prove themselves — regardless of what's happening off-screen.

What We Do

Host and organise BGMI tournaments — brackets, servers, rules, and prize distribution. We do not field a competing team.
