Privacy Policy

What we collect through this site, why, and what you can do about it.

What We Collect

When you submit the squad registration form, we collect: squad name, player IGNs, contact email, phone/WhatsApp number, Discord username, and any notes you choose to add. We don't collect anything through the rest of the site — there's no account system, no cookies, and no tracking scripts running here.

Why We Collect It

Solely to run tournaments — contacting squads about match schedules, verifying eligibility, and distributing prizes. We don't use this data for advertising, and we don't sell or rent it to anyone.

How It's Handled

Registration form submissions are delivered by email through FormSubmit, a third-party form-processing service. We don't currently maintain a separate database — submissions live in our email inbox and, where relevant, our Discord server (e.g. if you contact us there directly).

Third-Party Services

This site uses: FormSubmit (registration form delivery), Discord (community and support), Instagram (social presence), and Vercel (site hosting). Each operates under its own privacy policy, which governs any data they process on their platforms.

Your Rights

You can request a copy of the data we hold about your squad, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it entirely, by emailing us at the address in the footer below.

Changes

This policy may be updated as BeyondGød's tournament operations grow — for example, if we later adopt a dedicated registration platform or database. Check back before submitting future registrations.

This policy is a general-purpose template, not legal advice, and hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer. As BeyondGød starts handling real registrant data, especially for India-based users under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, we recommend having this reviewed by a qualified professional before relying on it for compliance.
