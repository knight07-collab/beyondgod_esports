Clear Space

Keep space around the mark equal to the height of the eagle's head — don't crowd it with other logos or text.

Colour

Use black on light backgrounds, white on dark, gold as the brand accent. Don't recolour the mark to anything else.

Don't

Don't stretch, rotate, add effects/outlines, or crop the wings — the mark must always appear whole.

Fan Use

Free for fan art, edits, and non-commercial posts with credit. Commercial/sponsor use requires written permission — contact us via Discord.
